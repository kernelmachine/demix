# SHIFT

A first-person puzzle game where you navigate through two overlapping dimensions.

## Concept

You exist in two parallel dimensions — **Red** and **Blue**. Press SPACE to shift between them. In the Red dimension, red walls are solid and blue walls are ghostly. In the Blue dimension, it's the opposite. Navigate through overlapping architecture by shifting at the right moments to reach the exit portal.

## Controls

- **WASD** — Move
- **Mouse** — Look around
- **SPACE** — Shift dimensions

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser
2. Click **ENTER** to start
3. Click the game to capture mouse
4. Navigate to the white portal to complete each level
5. Use dimension shifting to pass through colored walls

## Levels

1. **Introduction** — Learn the basic shift mechanic
2. **Double Shift** — Alternate between dimensions
3. **Dimensional Maze** — Pathfind through overlapping walls
4. **Multi-Room** — Plan your route across a grid of rooms
5. **The Gauntlet** — Rapid-fire dimension switching
6. **Open Arena** — Complex pathfinding with concentric barriers

## Tech

- Three.js for 3D rendering
- Pure JavaScript, no build step required
- Single HTML file, zero dependencies beyond the CDN import
